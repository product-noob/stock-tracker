## ⚠️ ALERT 1 — AAPL -2.10% to $331.21 (holding)

**What happened:** Post-earnings selloff, day two. Apple beat on fiscal Q4 last night — revenue $114.5B (+12% y/y), EPS $2.10 vs ~$1.99 consensus — but guided December-quarter gross margin to 45–46%, down from 47.9% in Q4, with Services growth slowing to 8–9% from 10%. Management called memory-price increases unprecedented and worsening, and disclosed a ~$33B jump in manufacturing purchase commitments. Shares fell 2.8% after hours Monday, opened down ~4% pre-market, and have recovered part of that to -2.1% mid-session. Analyst reaction is split, not panicked: Goldman cut its target $390 → $380 (Buy kept), Argus raised $375 → $400, HSBC cut $366 → $360, UBS raised $296 → $340 (Neutral kept).

**Koda's read:** The move looks justified on the margin guide, not on the quarter itself — the quarter was a beat, the guide is the problem, and memory costs are a real, multi-quarter headwind for the holiday quarter. But this is a guide-compression selloff, not a demand break: iPhone drove the beat, and the stock is still only ~6% off its $351.73 late-October high. Lean: **hold the holding; do not chase the dip yet** — the margin story needs a memory-price print or a December-quarter update before adding, and a retest toward the $325 intraday low would be a cleaner entry zone than catching it mid-fall. For the watchlist add, same view: wait for stabilisation, don't add into the slide.
