Context: AMZN had fallen ~5.6% over the prior two sessions ($254.60 → $240.35) after the Q3 miss and the ~$220B 2026 capex / "meaningfully more" in 2027 guidance, so part of today is an oversold bounce — the stock is still at/below its pre-selloff level.

So this looks like a justified relief bounce, not a new uptrend yet. Lean: **hold off chasing a +5% intraday spike**. Better entry setups are a pullback toward $240–245, or a close that holds above ~$255 with the tape's help. AAPL's print tonight could move the whole mega-cap tape either way, so patience costs nothing here.
