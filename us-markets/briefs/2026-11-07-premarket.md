### MSFT — Microsoft: $530.61, −0.99% Friday (week +1.67%)
- The only holding besides AAPL to close red on a bounce day; lagged the Nasdaq-100 by ~2.3 points.
- **Fresh, stock-specific:** Bloomberg (Nov 6, via Reuters) — Microsoft is winding down its 2023 arrangement to rent AI computing capacity from Oracle, declining to renew after the servers' older-generation Nvidia chips stopped meeting its needs; it has been switching the capacity off for months. This is part of a wider review of the $80B+ Microsoft has committed to renting capacity from outside providers (CoreWeave, Nscale, Nebius). Oracle fell ~3% on the report; Oracle says the capacity was renewed or resold at a ~20% premium. For Microsoft the read is cost discipline, not demand — it is choosing its own hardware over rented older GPUs — but it keeps the neocloud-oversight overhang alive into Monday.
- Watch: support $517.75; $530 is now the line to retake.
