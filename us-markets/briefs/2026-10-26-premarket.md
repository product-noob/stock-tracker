## META — the morning's story

Meta launched ~$35B in concurrent offerings this morning to fund AI infrastructure and personal superintelligence: $22.5B of Class A common stock via forward sales (Meta gets cash on settlement, by Nov 19, 2027 at the latest) plus ~$12.5B of depositary shares in mandatory convertible preferred stock (ticker to be METAM, converting ~Aug 2029). It also plans a separate up-to-$60B Class C at-the-market program starting Q1 2027 — not yet signed. Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom indicated non-binding interest in buying up to $5B combined of the Class A shares. Context: Meta's 2026 capex guide is $135–145B and Q3 free cash flow was −$2.12B. The stock spiked 2%+ early, then faded — dilution vs. AI-funding-certainty is the debate today. Source: Meta press release via Morningstar/PR Newswire; Stocktwits; Reuters.
