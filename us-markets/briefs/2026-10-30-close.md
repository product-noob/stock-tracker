## 🚨 GOOGL — $283.85, −12.5% (holding) — post-earnings brief

**The print (reported Wed after close):** Revenue $127.58B (+25% y/y) vs ~$128.2B consensus — a miss, the first since Q4 2022. EPS $2.12 vs ~$3.02 consensus, but the gap is mostly non-operating: an ~$8.7B unrealized loss on equity securities (Motley Fool identifies the SpaceX stake) plus a $5.2B cash EU antitrust fine payment. Google Cloud more than doubled (+103%) to $30.7B with a $561B backlog, and search query volume grew at its fastest in five years.

**Why the market sold it anyway:** Two real issues, not just the paper loss. (1) Search revenue growth slowed to ~12% from ~17% last quarter, which management blamed on currency, softer Asian retail ads, and lower monetization in AI Mode — the market's fear is that AI search earns less per query than classic search. (2) Cash: Q3 capex was $51.4B, free cash flow was −$9.07B, 2026 capex guidance ($195–205B) was kept, and 2027 spend is guided to rise "significantly" (some analysts model ~$300B). Alphabet funded the build with $12.4B of stock sales and $28.7B of new debt in the quarter. Volume was ~103M shares vs a ~21–31M average — capitulation-level turnover. Dow Jones Market Data: on pace for Alphabet's largest single-day percentage drop on record, a ~$400B+ market-value loss, and its lowest close since March.

**Koda's read:** The −12% looks overdone on the headline (a paper loss is not a broken business, and Cloud doubling with a $561B backlog is genuinely strong) but not baseless — negative FCF plus a search-monetization question justifies a de-rating. **Lean: HOLD — do not sell into a record capitulation day; any add waits for stabilization above the day's low (~$280) rather than catching the falling knife.** The thesis question to watch into Friday: do analysts' PT cuts stabilize, or does the "AI spend without payback" trade spread further?
