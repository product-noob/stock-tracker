## Why per notable mover

**NVDA +1.1% (record-high push).** Barron's flagged shares pushing toward a
new record close. Driver: AI-server partner Foxconn reported September-
quarter revenue of NT$3.03T (+47% y/y) and said AI-related operations would
keep growing in Q4 — a direct read-through to Blackwell/server demand.
All-time closing high to beat: $235.74 (May). Forward P/E ~17.1x per FactSet
(Barron's) — the setup remains "cheap for AI growth" even at $5.7T market cap.

**TSM +2.5% — the day's winner.** IBD's Stock of the Day. Two drivers:
(1) Elon Musk confirmed on X (Sat) that TSMC is in "discussions" about a
role in the Terafab chip project — Culpium/Tim Culpan reporting it would be a
TSMC-built/operated fab exclusively serving Tesla, SpaceX and xAI; Wedbush's
Matt Bryson reiterated Outperform; bad for Intel, which joined Terafab in
April. (2) Taiwan-listed shares hit a record NT$2,580 (+3.2%) on reports
Apple/Nvidia/AMD/Qualcomm/MediaTek raised 2nm orders 10–20%, with 2nm
output targeted at ~120k wafers/month by end-2026; JPMorgan raised its Taiwan
price target to NT$3,300. ADR intraday record high $486.57–487.47. Q3
earnings Oct 15 (consensus ~$4.46 EPS / $45.55B revenue, per earlier preview).

**MSFT +1.6%.** Melius Research upgraded from Hold to Buy with a **$665
target** (Oct 5), citing enterprise demand for AI security and governance;
F4Q showed 43% Azure growth and $678B commercial RPO (+84%) per the report
roundup. Watch: Oct 7 Windows/Surface event with Nvidia's Jensen Huang
(1 PM ET, RTX Spark debut).

**META +2.0%** (watchlist; no fresh catalyst). Continuation momentum from the
Connect cycle; 52-week high $779.82 still ~4.9% above. Regulatory overhangs
live this week (Warren tax-inquiry responses due; FTC AI-agent probe from
Sept 30) — kept out of the flag list again for no single driver. Earnings
~Oct 28.

**ASML −0.5% (diverged from TSM).** Quiet session on the ADR; Euronext quote
finished ~flat (−0.2%) at €1,658.10. Overhang: possible US export-control
moves (Dutch PM Jetten raised it with Trump; CEO Fouquet pushed back on
tighter curbs in the FT). Support from the buyback program (165,200 shares /
€248.2M in the Sept 21–25 window). Earnings Oct 14 — the next real test.

**AMZN flat.** Prime Big Deal Days starts Tuesday (Oct 6–7): 48 hours, 35
categories; Adobe projects $10B in US spend during the window and ~$96B for
October. Bernstein notes US e-commerce spending +~10% y/y in H1 2026 after
two years of mid-single-digit growth. Earnings ~Oct 29.

**GOOGL +0.9%.** Part of the broad megacap bid. Background: JPMorgan $420
target (Oct 1); Gemini 4 Argon rolling out to select orgs pending safety
testing; Cloud backlog $514B and FY26 capex guidance raised to $195–205B.
Earnings ~Nov 4.

**AAPL −0.1%.** Quiet day. No fresh catalyst; named among the 60+ stocks in
the OKX/ICE tokenized-stock filing (24/7 blockchain trading proposal).

**Also of note:** OKX + NYSE-owner ICE filed plans for a 24/7 tokenized
stock market — the filing names NVDA, TSLA, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL and 60+
others (CoinDesk, Oct 5). Companies can object within 30 days; Cerebras
already did. Neutral-to-mild positive sentiment item, not a price driver.
