## Why today moved

**The October jobs report smashed expectations.** Nonfarm payrolls +165,000 vs ~65,000 expected; unemployment fell to 4.1% from 4.2%; average hourly earnings +0.4% m/m and +3.1% y/y, both above forecasts; August and September payrolls were revised up by a combined 84,000 (TradingView/Benzinga). The strong print lifted December Fed hike odds to ~76–77% (CME FedWatch) and the 10-year yield to ~4.88–4.89% — a headwind the tape mostly ignored while dip buyers worked. Fed's Mary Daly called the labour market solid and the report good news that gives the Fed room to keep fighting inflation (XTB).

**University of Michigan sentiment actually improved** — preliminary November reading 51 vs 48 in October, beating expectations, and one-year inflation expectations eased to 4.3% from 4.7%, the lowest since August (TradingView). Correction to the mid-session brief, which framed the 51 print as a crater: 48 → 51 is an improvement, and inflation expectations fell, not rose.

**TSM had a second, stock-specific driver** (see below): The Information reported, via Reuters, that TSMC is drawing up plans to produce advanced memory chips — specifically 3D DRAM — at Nvidia's request, led by VP Hsu Kuo-chin. No timeline, no capex figure, no fab plan; TSMC and Nvidia declined to comment.

**Elsewhere on the tape:** Lam Research +8% after announcing a $25B buyback; AMD, Intel and Micron +6–10%; Moderna +~5% and Vistra +~10% on earnings; Airbnb +10.4% after raising its full-year 2026 revenue growth forecast.

## Koda's read

A relief bounce, not a resolution. The afternoon fade (MSFT and AAPL slipping red, META giving back nearly half its gain) says buyers were renting the bounce, not re-rating the AI buildout. Nothing in today's move changes the watch levels from the mid-session check: NVDA $195–200 resistance / $181–185 floor, TSM $378–380 resistance, ASML $1,450 stabilisation, META $600.
