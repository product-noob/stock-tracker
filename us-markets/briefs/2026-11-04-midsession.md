## The tape in one line

Democrats swept both chambers of Congress in the Nov 3 midterms, and the market's read flipped overnight from panic to rally: divided government = gridlock = less new spending/borrowing, and — the trade that matters for this portfolio — curbs on new data-center construction. Cash-generating mega-caps surged on the idea that a slower AI buildout means less capex burn and recovering free cash flow; the suppliers of that buildout fell. S&P 500 +1.56%, Dow +2.28%, Nasdaq-100 +1.41% (Barchart). Oct ADP jobs +86k (vs +67k expected) and a narrower Sep trade deficit (-$105.0B) also supported the tape.

## ⚠️ ALERT — AMZN +6.9% to a 3-month high

**What happened:** The biggest gainer of the five mega-caps (Reuters: +7.4% intraday at one point). The market's logic: Amazon's ~$220B 2026 capex plan was the single biggest worry hanging over the stock after its Oct 29 earnings; if data-center expansion slows — Texas governor-elect pledged a moratorium, and voters in Ohio, Illinois, Maine and Florida passed local bans/restrictions (Dow Jones via Morningstar) — the cash-burn fear eases and FCF recovers.

**Koda's read:** The FCF-relief logic is real, but this is a policy *narrative*, not enacted law — a moratorium pledge is not a moratorium. A 7% one-day jump on politics is not an entry signal. Lean: **hold**; for a watchlist add, wait to see whether the move holds through the week rather than chasing today's spike. (Analysis only, not financial advice.)

## ⚠️ ALERT — MSFT +6.9% to a new high (above its $551.15 52-week high)

**What happened:** Same gridlock/data-center thesis, and Microsoft was already the exception in the group — $18.6B quarterly FCF in its Oct 28 print while peers went cash-negative. A slower buildout eases its capex burden without touching its Azure growth story.

**Koda's read:** Of today's five gainers, MSFT has the strongest fundamental backing for the move — it was the cash-flow-strong name before today. Lean: **hold**; nothing about today's rally changes the holding thesis, and a new high after a 6.9% day is not a moment to add aggressively. (Analysis only, not financial advice.)

## ⚠️ ALERT — AAPL +6.5% to a record high

**What happened:** Apple avoided the hyperscaler capex trap entirely, and it has a second tailwind in the same thesis: if data-center demand cools, the memory-price surge that pressured its Dec-quarter gross-margin guide (45–46%) eases too. Evercore also flags Apple's defensive characteristics in this tape. Reuters: +~7% to a record high; the five mega-caps are on course to add $750B+ in combined market value today.

**Koda's read:** The most "justified" of today's jumps — two tailwinds, no capex overhang — but 6.5% in a day on an election read is still a lot, and the Dec-q margin guide hasn't changed. Lean: **hold**; do not chase a record high on day one of a political narrative. (Analysis only, not financial advice.)
