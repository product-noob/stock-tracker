## Weekend developments (US sources only)

- **White House voluntary AI safety accord (signed Tuesday):** NVDA, GOOGL and
  META signed; MSFT and AMZN leaders attended the lunch but did not sign; AAPL
  was not among the attendees. One-page pledge — internal controls, independent
  audits, board-level audit committees; no penalties or deadline (Stocktwits
  Mag-7 weekly wrap).
- **Sen. Warren tax inquiry (CNBC reporting, weekend coverage):** letters to
  the CEOs of GOOGL, MSFT, AMZN and META demanding detail on AI/data-center
  tax breaks (~$96B figure per ITEP-sourced reporting) and lobbying around the
  One Big Beautiful Bill Act's 100% bonus depreciation. Responses due Oct 11 —
  a headline risk to watch for all four this week.
- **NVDA:** announced a $4,999 64GB DGX Spark config (models to 100B params,
  on sale Oct 23 via Acer/ASUS/Dell/Gigabyte/HP/MSI). RTX Spark is slated to
  debut at Microsoft's Windows/Surface event on **Oct 7**, with Jensen Huang
  expected in person — one to watch for MSFT + NVDA tape. Friday was a new
  all-time closing high ($236.36); Seeking Alpha noted the 96-trading-day
  streak without a new high finally broke.
- **AAPL:** said macOS will require "very explicit user action" before an app
  gets Full Disk Access — explicitly framed around AI agents' growing access
  risks, after a privacy complaint about Meta's Muse Mac app. Minor platform
  item; watch for developer-reaction chatter Monday.
- **AMZN:** pledged $1B over five years to communities around its data
  centers and said it no longer uses NDAs with local governments, answering
  the data-center pushback wave. The ~$8B Grace Blackwell SPV/leaseback
  (FT reporting) remains the AWS balance-sheet story.
- **TSM / ASML:** no fresh company news this weekend; Samsung High-NA EUV
  partnership, Intel's 1M+ High-NA wafers processed, and TSMC's 2030 High-NA
  volume plan remain the backdrop. TSM reports Oct 15, ASML Oct 14.
