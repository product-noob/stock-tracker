## Koda's read

This was a macro-plus-one-headline selloff, not a thesis break.
Oil (+4% to ~$104 Brent) and a 5.3%+ 10Y set the multiple in the
bond pit again, and the FT's OpenAI revenue revision (~$50B vs
~$70B signaled) gave the chip complex a specific reason to de-risk
into earnings season: NVDA −2.9%, TSM −3.0%, ASML −1.9% are the
same trade at three points of the supply chain. The important
distinction: TSM's September sales (+55% YoY) and Samsung's record
profit forecast both say physical demand is not rolling over —
prices fell because expectations and positioning are stretched,
which Barron's $240-call buying into GTC underlines. AAPL +1.1%
on a product event and META flat show this was not indiscriminate
megacap selling. Late-session relief (Trump/Iran post, in-line
30Y auction, yields off highs) kept the S&P proxy to −0.5%, but
the Nasdaq-100 at −1.3% says tech took the hit. Nothing today
changes the calendar that matters: **ASML Oct 14, TSM Oct 15, CPI
Oct 14, FOMC Oct 28–29.** No buys or sells triggered by today's
tape alone; NVDA/TSM approaching the sudden-move alert band on a
sector headline is flagged here rather than as a name-specific
alert, per the rule.
