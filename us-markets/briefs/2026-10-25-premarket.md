## The weekend headline: Iran escalation risk into Monday's open

President Trump said Saturday in Florida that the US could launch fresh strikes on Iran as soon as Sunday ("maybe tomorrow") — or reach a deal, leaving both paths open. Reports that Iran offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for lifting the US naval blockade were denied by Tehran. That binary — strikes or a deal, possibly within hours — is the single biggest swing factor for Monday's open, via oil: Brent already settled Friday at a six-month high (see correction below), and any Hormuz headline moves crude first, equities second.
