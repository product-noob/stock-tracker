## 🚨 GOOGL — $284.75, −12.3% (holding)

**What happened:** Alphabet reported Q3 after Wednesday's close and missed for the first time since Q4 2022: EPS $2.12 vs ~$3.02 consensus, revenue $127.58B vs ~$128.2B consensus. The EPS shortfall was amplified by an ~$8.7B unrealized loss on equity securities (Motley Fool identifies the SpaceX stake) plus a $5.2B cash EU antitrust fine payment. Free cash flow was −$9.07B in the quarter, and 2027 capex is guided to rise "significantly" from the $195–205B 2026 range. On the other side, Google Cloud revenue more than doubled (+103%) to $30.7B, and search query volume grew at its fastest in five years — but search revenue growth slowed to ~12% from ~17% last quarter (Invezz), which is what the market seized on. Shares fell as much as ~13% intraday, on course for Alphabet's worst post-earnings drop on record and a ~$410B market-value loss (Motley Fool).

**Koda's read:** The move looks overdone on the headline but not baseless. A large part of the EPS miss is a non-operating paper loss, not a broken operating business — Cloud doubling is genuinely strong. What is real: negative FCF plus rising capex, with search monetization slowing as AI Mode expands, gives the "AI spending without payback" trade fresh ammunition. That justifies a de-rating, not a verdict on the franchise. **Lean: HOLD — do not sell into a −12% capitulation day; any add should wait for stabilization above the day's low (~$280) rather than catching the falling knife.** Watch the close and whether analysts' PT cuts (Invezz notes cuts but continued bullishness) stabilize sentiment.

## 🚨 META — $633.74, +3.6% (watchlist)

**What happened:** No fresh company news. META is bouncing after a five-session post-earnings losing streak that had taken it from ~$697 to ~$612. The day's catalyst is Bill Ackman on CNBC calling the stock "incredibly undervalued," citing Meta's ~$80B cash position and lack of net leverage to fund AI spend (TradingPedia). It is also a relative-rotation day: with GOOGL being sold on AI-spend fears, money is rotating into the mega-cap that already took its post-earnings medicine — Yahoo's midday headline frames it as "Alphabet sinks, Microsoft and Meta edge higher."

**Koda's read:** A relief bounce with a credible valuation voice behind it, not a fundamental re-rating — META is still well below its ~$667 50-day average and its Q3 capex guide ($135–145B) hasn't changed. **Lean: HOLD on entry timing — the bounce is real but chasing +3.6% after a 5-day slide is paying up for an Ackman headline; a better entry would be a hold above ~$630 into the close or a retest of ~$612.** The oversold setup (RSI ~31 per third-party data) supports patience over chasing.

AMZN opened down ~3.8% and is caught in the GOOGL-led AI-spend selloff (Longbridge) plus pre-earnings caution: it reports Q3 **after tonight's close** (call 5 PM ET).
