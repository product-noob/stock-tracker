## Apple — the story of the morning (post-earnings)

Apple reported fiscal Q4 after Monday's close — its first quarter under CEO John Ternus. The quarter beat, the guide didn't, and the stock is paying for the guide.

- **Reported:** revenue $114.5B (+12% y/y, vs ~$113.4B consensus), diluted EPS $2.10 (vs ~$1.99), net income $30.7B — a September-quarter record. iPhone $57.58B (+17%, above ~$56.5B est.), Services a record $31.6B (+10%), Mac $10.8B (+24%). Gross margin 47.9%, including ~1pt from tariff refunds (+$0.05 EPS).
- **Guided (December quarter):** revenue growth 7–10% (a record holiday quarter in dollars, but below the ~11% some expected), Services growth 8–9% (vs ~10.7% expected), and gross margin 45–46% vs 46.9% expected. CFO Kevan Parekh tied the margin squeeze to rising memory costs plus Siri AI ramp costs; Ternus called memory pricing "unprecedented" and said costs keep rising past December. Greater China ($17.54B vs ~$18.75B est.) was the quarter's soft spot; Apple Intelligence testing in China starts Nov 4 on Alibaba's Qwen models.
- **Pre-market:** $325.26, −3.9% vs Monday's $338.32 close (other snapshots put it −4.2 to −4.7% through the morning; Barron's: Nvidia retakes the world's-most-valuable crown at the open if this holds — ~$4.94T vs Apple's ~$4.71T).
- **Watch:** whether $325 holds into the open; reclaiming the $338 close is the first sign the sell-off is just guidance de-risking, not a re-rating. Memory-cost commentary is now the swing factor for the whole hardware chain.
