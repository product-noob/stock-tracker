## 📊 Pre-earnings brief: AAPL — Monday Nov 2, after close (call 5:00 PM ET)

The week's main event — and the one mega-cap without an AI-capex problem reports into a tape that has just punished every name that does have one.

- **Consensus:** EPS ~$1.98–1.99 (MarketBeat $1.99, Zacks $1.98) on revenue ~$113.4B — about +7.6% EPS and +10.7% revenue vs a year ago ($1.85 / $102.47B). Apple has beaten EPS consensus in each of its last 8 reports. Options imply a ~4% post-print move.
- **Apple's own guide (from the Jul 30 call):** revenue growth +9% to +11% (~$111.7–113.7B), iPhone revenue up mid-teens, Services roughly flat vs the June quarter, and gross margin 47–48%, including ~1 point of tariff refunds — so ex-refunds the underlying margin guide is ~46–47%.
- **What to watch:** (1) The December-quarter guide — the first outlook from new CEO John Ternus (succeeded Tim Cook on Sep 1), for a holiday quarter that carried $85.3B of iPhone a year ago. Street expects ~$152.6B revenue (+6.1%) and a 46.3% gross margin; Goldman expects Apple to guide low-to-mid single-digit growth, below consensus (its own Dec-quarter estimates: $151.2B revenue, $2.88 EPS, 46.8% margin; Goldman raised its PT to $390 from $360). (2) Memory costs — DRAM/NAND prices rose in March, June and again in September as AI data centers compete for capacity; inventory doubled to $11.09B in nine months, and Apple says that carry-in benefit fades after September. (3) iPhone vs Services — iPhone Duo demand was called "extraordinary" with shipping into Jan 2027; iPhone revenue is forecast +15.3% to ~$56.5B; Services momentum has to offset any iPhone softness. (4) The margin read: consensus sits near the top of Apple's revenue guide, so a midpoint print could still read as a miss.
- **Levels:** Friday's range was $334.35–$349.19; the stock held well above its ~$332 50-day average and closed ~$344 — the most resilient mega-cap into earnings, and now the world's most valuable company again.
