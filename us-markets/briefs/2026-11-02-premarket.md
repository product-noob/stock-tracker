## 📊 Pre-earnings brief: AAPL — TODAY, after the close (call 5:00 PM ET)

The penultimate Magnificent Seven report (only NVDA is left after this), and the first earnings call led by new CEO John Ternus, who succeeded Tim Cook on Sep 1.

- **Consensus:** EPS ~$1.99 (range $1.98–2.00 across Bloomberg/TipRanks/MarketBeat) on revenue ~$113.4B (range ~$112.95–113.46B) — about +7.6% EPS and +10.7% revenue vs a year ago ($1.85 / $102.47B). Options imply a ~±4% post-print move (Nov 4 straddle, max pain $350).
- **Segment expectations:** iPhone ~$56.5B (+~15% y/y), Services ~$31.6–31.7B, Mac ~$9.69B, Wearables ~$8.99B, iPad ~$6.83B. Apple's own guide (Jul 30 call): revenue +9–11%, gross margin 47–48% including ~1 point of tariff refunds — so the underlying margin guide is nearer 46–47%, down from 50.1% in the June quarter as memory costs rise and tariff-refund benefits fade.
- **What to watch:** (1) The December-quarter guide — the holiday quarter, and the first outlook from Ternus; Street expects ~$152.6B revenue, while Goldman expects a low-to-mid single-digit guide, below consensus. (2) iPhone 18 demand colour — the September quarter only caught early iPhone 18 Pro sales; iPhone Duo (on sale Oct 23) lands in the December quarter, so management's demand commentary matters more than the print itself. (3) Memory costs — DRAM/NAND prices rose through 2026 as AI data centers compete for capacity; watch what Ternus says about the December-quarter margin. (4) Services momentum, which has to offset any hardware softness.
- **Levels:** Friday's range $334.35–349.19; the stock held above its ~$332 50-day average and closed as the world's most valuable company again (~$5.03T, ahead of NVDA ~$4.93T).
