## Koda's read

META's quarter was exactly the "good enough" the de-risked tape needed: ad growth intact (+~26% YoY), Q4 guide above street, and a 2026 capex guide raised only at the low end — the market's +3% after-hours vote says the feared $10B-settlement-plus-AI-overspend combo didn't break the model. But the real question never was Q3: it's 2027 capex (street expects ~$196B) and the new Class C vehicle signals Meta knows it needs fresh capital, not just internal cash flow. Don't re-rate on the print alone — wait for the call's 2027 color. Elsewhere, the day was rotation-confirming: ASML and NVDA on genuine new catalysts (Terafab acceleration, DSX POD), while GOOGL's two-day drift is the tape's soft spot into its Oct 28 print — worth a level-based plan ($330) rather than emotion.
