## What drove the day

- **The reversal:** Wednesday's rally (Dow +3.03%, its best day since April 2025; S&P +1.58%; Nasdaq +1.44%) was built on the Democrats' midterm sweep and the gridlock thesis. Thursday the market priced the other side of that same thesis: a Democratic Congress is now expected to constrain data-centre construction (permitting, power, local restrictions), which hits the AI supply chain first (Invezz; Reuters wire).
- **Qualcomm's warning shot:** fiscal Q4 handset-chip revenue fell 26% year over year to $5.14B, below the ~$5.25B expected, and total revenue fell 8% to $10.32B — QCOM fell ~13% and dragged the whole semiconductor complex with it.
- **Valuation doubt:** the "AI bubble" debate resurfaced (Michael Burry's warnings circulating again), with financing risk around AI infrastructure debt back in focus (Invezz). The SOX's −11.5% intraday low puts it on course for its worst day since the March 2020 COVID crash.
- **No universe earnings today.** No holding or watchlist name reported. The day's numbers are politics, positioning and a demand scare — not fundamentals.
