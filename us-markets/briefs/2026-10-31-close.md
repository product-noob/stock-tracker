## 📊 AMZN — ~$240, ~−1.8% (watchlist) — post-earnings brief

**The print (reported Thu after close):** Revenue $201.94B (+12% y/y) vs ~$202.2B consensus — a narrow miss. EPS $1.89 vs ~$1.97 consensus. The miss was in the consumer businesses, not the cloud: online-store sales grew just 2% to $68.5B (vs ~$69.7B expected, slowest since early 2023) and advertising grew ~11–12% to $19.6B (vs ~$20.8B expected). AWS was the bright spot: $45.98B, +39.3% y/y, its fastest growth in nearly five years, with Jassy guiding AWS growth above 40% in both 2027 and 2028.

**Why the market sold it anyway:** Cash, not demand. Q3 property-and-equipment capex hit $77.3B (vs ~$35.1B a year ago, ~$20B above estimates), 2026 capex is ~$220B, and Jassy said 2027 will be "meaningfully" higher. Trailing free cash flow is now −$42.4B (vs +$14.8B a year ago). Q4 guidance — revenue $240–247B (midpoint just under ~$244.5B consensus) and operating income $28.5–33.5B (midpoint $31B vs ~$33B consensus) — gave no offset. The stock fell ~7% after hours Thu to ~$226, opened Fri at $232.54, printed a $229.36 low, and recovered to ~$240 by late session — a V-shape off the lows, but still its second straight down day. Volume was heavy (~68M vs ~46M average on MarketBeat's late read). Analysts mostly held the line: Goldman reiterated Conviction-Buy ($370), BofA reiterated Buy, Truist set $390 — while Citizens JMP cut its target $315 → $275.

**Koda's read:** The quarter's demand story is genuinely strong (AWS accelerating, Jassy's >40% guide for 2027–28), so this is a cash-burn re-rating, not a broken-business selloff — the same distinction as GOOGL on Thursday. **Lean: HOLD on entry timing — the bounce off ~$226 shows dip buyers are present, but the 2027 capex debate hangs over the stock into next week; a staggered entry only on weakness toward the $226–230 zone looks better than chasing today's recovery.** The thesis question into Monday: does the AWS >40% growth guide start to outweigh the capex math, or does the "spend first, profit later" discount deepen?

## GOOGL — $278.80, −1.8% (holding)

## NVDA — ~$202, ~−5.3% (holding)

The chip rout's biggest mega-cap casualty after Intel. Intel guided Q4 revenue $15–16B (vs $17.07B consensus), warned PC demand stays weak into H1 2027, and said it does not expect positive cash flow this year — Intel fell ~30.7% to $63.77 and dragged AMD (−9.5%), Broadcom, Micron and the server makers with it. Nvidia-specific headline: The Information reported Nvidia is in talks to guarantee billions of dollars of Nscale debt for a 460 MW Anthropic data center in West Virginia (Nvidia already guarantees an Nscale Texas lease and has committed $1B to the company) — exactly the kind of AI-credit exposure the market is punishing right now. Nvidia's market cap dipped below $5T intraday. Volume ~114M (stockanalysis late print) vs ~78M Thursday. **Lean: HOLD — nothing in Intel's PC warning changes Nvidia's AI demand picture, and Nvidia reports ~Nov 17; no reason to sell a holding on a peer's warning, and any add waits for the tape to stabilize.**
