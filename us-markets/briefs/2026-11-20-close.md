## The day's story: the Anthropic pop didn't save the tape

**Anthropic (ANT) debuted — and faded like everything else.** The Claude maker opened at **$111.50 (+34% vs the $83 IPO price)**, briefly touched ~$115 and a ~$2T valuation, then slid to ~$95–100 by early afternoon (+15–20% vs IPO). The largest IPO on record ($81.3B, 980M shares) was supposed to revive the AI trade; instead the whole tape opened +1–2% and reversed within the first hour. By noon the S&P had erased a +1.3% early gain (AP).

**Why the reversal — four drivers, none of them new:**
1. **The Fed is the brake.** Vice Chair Jefferson said the AI buildout is adding to inflation; Chicago Fed's Goolsbee warned the Fed's job is to prevent the AI investment surge from overheating the economy and that tariff inflation looks more persistent than expected. December hike ~95% priced (25bp), with Waller still floating 50bp.
2. **Soft morning data.** Philly Fed manufacturing fell to **6.3** (6-month low) vs ~20 expected (prior 20.9), with prices-received at its highest since June 2022. Jobless claims **203k** vs ~198k expected. Stagflationary flavour — weak activity, sticky prices.
3. **Semis took a second hit.** AMD fell ~7–8% after short-seller Hunterbrook alleged China export-control violations — the chip tape never recovered.
4. **Earnings disappointments outside tech.** Walmart beat and raised guidance but fell ~1.4% (Q4 implied EPS below Street); UnitedHealth extended the managed-care selloff (~-4%) on the CMS Medicare Advantage clawback proposal; Williams-Sonoma -13.5% on a revenue miss.
