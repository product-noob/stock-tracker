## Weekend chip tape — big for TSM, NVDA, ASML

**Samsung to use TSMC's 3nm for Nvidia's NVHBM (Reuters, Seoul, Oct 18).** Seoul Economic Daily: Samsung will produce base dies for Nvidia's next-gen custom HBM (NVHBM, unveiled August) on TSMC's 3-nanometre process — the **first time Samsung has used TSMC at the HBM manufacturing stage**. Nvidia has asked Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron to use TSMC's process for NVHBM base dies and 6th-gen DRAM for core dies. Samsung accepted after internal review but keeps in-house production for standard HBM. Read-through: incremental 3nm wafer demand for TSM; supply-chain standardization around NVHBM de-risks Nvidia's next memory ramp.

**ASML "sold out" — demanding painful down payments.** Post-earnings color: CFO Roger Dassen says down payments on orders are "enough to hurt" if customers cancel; ASML is **holding off accepting 2029 and 2030 orders** while it assesses customer readiness and its own capacity. TSMC maintained its 2026 capex at $60–64B; Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron are booking ASML capacity for AI memory. China expected ~20% of 2026 sales. (europesays, Oct 18)
