## Alerts — Koda's read

### TSM −4.54% (~$356.49) — ALERT
**What happened:** Second straight day of the Asia-led chip rout. Samsung and TSMC fell ~5% in Asian trading after the Commerce/BIS interim final rule (Reuters): licences now required for advanced AI chip shipments to data centres in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Brazil, plus tighter due-diligence/licensing for foundries making advanced chips for Chinese fabless firms. Taiwan's Taiex fell again; South Korea's Kospi hit a six-month low. The ADR broke straight through the $368 support flagged yesterday — intraday low $353.50.
**Is it justified?** Partly. The rule is real and it lands directly on TSMC's customer base, but nothing about TSMC's own business changed: record October revenue NT$586.89B (+59.7% y/y), Q4 guide $52.3–54.3B, and the stock is now ~18% below its record high with a 2027 P/E near 18x (Motley Fool). This is a sentiment-and-positioning washout into the NVDA print, not a fundamentals break.
**Koda's read — HOLD / watch for entry:** The $368–375 scale-in zone from yesterday is now above the price; the first scale-in zone moves to $350–360 — but only after tonight's NVDA print. If the print disappoints, the next support is the low-$340s. Don't catch the knife before the call.

### MSFT −3.98% (~$497.40) — ALERT (new today)
**What happened:** The day's biggest mover in the universe, and the first time Microsoft has led the selloff. It broke the $515–516 support that had held repeatedly, then the $500 psychological level (intraday low $496.51). Three drivers: (1) preliminary findings released Monday on last week's global Microsoft 365 outage — the bug had lurked undetected in Microsoft's data centres since July, and routine traffic rerouting on Nov 11 cascaded into failures across Outlook, Teams and Copilot; full service was only restored Nov 16, with the final review due Nov 20 (Business Times); (2) the broad AI-tape selloff into NVDA earnings; (3) Ignite opened today (Nadella keynote 1:00 PM ET) — Copilot/Azure announcements so far have not steadied the stock. 5-session return is now -8.76%; the stock is ~11% below its Nov 4 high of $559.15.
**Is it justified?** Overdone on the day, but not baseless. The outage is a reliability dent on the cloud story — exactly the kind of thing enterprise customers remember — and it lands while the whole AI-capex thesis is being repriced. Nothing in it breaks the demand picture: Q1 FY27 beat, Azure +48%, FCF $18.6B.
**Koda's read — HOLD:** No change to the position. The outage is a reputational hit, not a demand break, and $497 is ~19x forward earnings territory for the steadiest cash-flow name in the book. But don't add into a falling tape before the NVDA print — $490–500 is the next test, and ex-dividend is Thursday Nov 19 ($0.98).
