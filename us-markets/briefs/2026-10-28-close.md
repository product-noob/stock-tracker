# Market-close recap — Tuesday Oct 27, 2026 (4:00 PM ET)

**Chips carried the tape for a second straight day; Meta kept falling.** Tuesday's catalyst was financing, not a filing: after Monday's close, Bank of America, Blackstone, Citi and Morgan Stanley announced a **$63 billion committed financing package for a vehicle that buys Broadcom XPU accelerators and networking gear and leases them to Anthropic**, with deliveries from calendar 2027 (AP via market coverage; Investing.com). That answered, for a day at least, the AI-funding question that has dogged the tape for weeks — banks will lend against AI hardware, with Broadcom backing the vehicle with residual-value support. **Broadcom rose ~4%** and was, with Nvidia, one of the two biggest contributors to the S&P 500's advance (AP). The **S&P 500 closed at 7,722.05 (+~0.6% vs Monday's 7,676.77)** and the **Nasdaq Composite at 26,447.40 (+~1.1% vs 26,169.38)**; the Nasdaq-100 CFD proxy rose ~+1.2%. Most S&P stocks actually fell — this was a heavyweight-led advance, not a broad one. Macro helped at the margin: **consumer confidence jumped to 91.7 in October from 84.2 in September, beating the ~81 expected** (Conference Board via Morningstar), and WTI crude eased to ~$100 (−1.7%) with the 10-year yield near 5.00%.

Tomorrow is the week's main event: **FOMC decision at 2 PM ET, then GOOGL and MSFT after the close, plus Apple's "Pro and tell" event at 10 AM PT.**

## Why each name moved

**TSM +2.89% ($436.64) — second straight flagged day, still sector beta.** No TSMC-specific news; the ADR again outran its home listing (Taiwan-listed TSMC +0.63% overnight to NT$2,400, per Focus Taiwan/CNA). The mid-session flag (+2.86% at 1 PM ET) held into the close. The Anthropic-financing headlines lift the whole AI-hardware chain, and TSMC is the foundry at the centre of it — but nothing in today's move is new TSMC information. Q3 strength (record $46.7B revenue, 66.4% gross margin, Q4 guide $52.3–54.3B) remains the fundamental anchor.

**ASML +2.77% ($1,634.89) — same driver, ex-div resilience again.** No ASML-specific news; today was the ex-dividend date (EUR 1.88, payable Nov 4), which normally drags the price — rising ~2.8% through it, for a second day, underlines how strong the sector bid is. The ADR is now comfortably above its ~$1,586 200-day moving average, reclaimed on Monday. Thesis unchanged since the Oct 14 Q3 print (2027–28 supply scarcity, FY26 guide raised to EUR 44–45B).

**AMZN +1.71% ($253.36) — drifting up into Thursday's earnings.** No company news strong enough to explain the move on its own; it rode the tech bid plus a UBS target raise to $348 (from $318) noted today. One overhang surfaced: Business Insider reported an August internal Amazon forecast of a ~$612M gross loss on devices this year on rising memory costs (Amazon declined to comment) — a hardware-margin story, not an AWS one, and the stock rose through it. Earnings Thu Oct 29 after close (call 5 PM ET): cons EPS ~$1.96–1.97, rev ~$202.2B vs guide $197–202B, op inc $22.5–26.5B; watch AWS growth and the ~$220B capex run-rate.

**GOOGL +1.49% ($321.51) — first green day after 7 straight down days, and back above $320.** Relief bounce into tomorrow's earnings (AMC, FOMC day), not a reversal signal on its own — the day's low was $316.86, so it dipped under $320 intraday again before recovering. No fresh company news. Earnings Wed Oct 28 after close (call 4:30 PM ET): cons adj EPS ~$3.03, rev ~$127.9B; watch Cloud growth, Search, and capex/FCF. This is the highest-stakes print in the universe tomorrow.

**AAPL +0.91% ($347.03) — steady near the highs into tomorrow's event.** No fresh company news; the move retraced Monday's −1.11% drift. Tomorrow's Oct 28 "Pro and tell" event (10 AM PT; Bloomberg: first touch-screen MacBook Pro expected) is the near-term catalyst, with earnings Nov 2 after close behind it. The iPhone Duo ($1,999) demand story is unchanged.

**NVDA +0.86% ($217.85) — quiet despite being the story's centre.** Broadcom, not Nvidia, was Tuesday's AI-financing winner (+~4%); Nvidia held Monday's break above $215 and traded a tight $215.53–219.19 range. The Meta offering angle also continued: Meta's prospectus names Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom as cornerstone investors indicating interest in up to an aggregate $5B of the $22.5B Class A offering (per the SEC filing) — a $3B Nvidia slice was reported Monday. Small relative to Nvidia's scale; the market treated it as background. Earnings ~Nov 17.

**MSFT −0.24% ($531.31) — flat into tomorrow's earnings.** The quietest mega-cap day in the universe: no fresh company news, a $528.33–534.70 range, and a small give-back of Monday's +1.49%. It remains the best-behaving AI mega-cap into earnings. Earnings Wed Oct 28 after close (call 5:30 PM ET): cons adj EPS ~$4.71, rev ~$90.6B; watch Azure ~+45% constant-currency and capex >$50B.

**META −1.60% ($620.49) — dilution still winning over partnership headlines.** Down for the session despite the Nvidia cornerstone-investor news and despite the chip tape rallying — the market continues to treat the ~$35B offerings plus the planned up-to-$60B Class C program as dilution first. Day low $619.21, now well below the 2026 open (~$648.74). Post-earnings drift continues (Q3 beat: rev $64.6B, Q4 guide $73–76B, capex $135–145B); the $25B New Mexico privacy penalty appeal and the Jan 19 Class C distribution remain overhangs.

## Benchmark context

This was a **narrow, heavyweight-led advance**: the S&P +~0.6% / Nasdaq +~1.1% day was carried by AI-linked heavyweights (Broadcom, Nvidia) while most S&P constituents fell. Against that tape, TSM, ASML and AMZN beat the benchmarks; GOOGL roughly matched the Nasdaq; AAPL and NVDA lagged it; MSFT was flat; META lagged outright. In other words, the chip chain paid again, the ad/AI-platform names did not — the same split as Monday, now two days running.

## Koda's read

Two days of chip-led gains have repaired last week's damage without changing any thesis — both days were Anthropic headlines (filing, then financing), not company results. The read into tomorrow is unchanged from Monday's: hold the breakout levels (NVDA $215, TSM $418, ASML $1,586) and let GOOGL/MSFT earnings plus the FOMC decision decide the next move. GOOGL reclaiming $320 into its own print is a slightly better setup than Monday's break below it — but a miss on FOMC day can still hand it straight back. No new action ahead of tomorrow's prints.
