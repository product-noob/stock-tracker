## The story: Nvidia's board is weighing Jensen Huang's future

Overnight, the NYT reported a second alleged extramarital affair — this time with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's VP of healthcare, who reports directly to Huang — three days after Huang publicly acknowledged a first affair on Nov 8. Reports (NYT, Reuters, AP, CNBC) say the board met late Wednesday and is preparing to remove Huang, possibly deciding as soon as today; no decision had been announced as of this morning. The board says Huang will cooperate with the investigation and retains confidence in him.

- NVDA fell ~8.6% in after-hours trading Wednesday, on top of Wednesday's +2.87% relief bounce.
- Wall Street is moving fast: William Blair downgraded to Market Perform; Bernstein cut to Market Perform with PT $400 → $200; Baird cut to Neutral, PT $500 → $200; Citi cut PT to $225, Stifel to $205; Morgan Stanley removed NVDA from its top-picks list. Dan Ives put it at $225 without Huang vs $300 with him. Morningstar held its $310 fair value, forecasts unchanged.
- Prediction markets (Polymarket) put ~75% odds on Huang leaving before end-2026.
- The FBI/board probes and House briefing (Nov 18) and the Nov 17 earnings report are still live overhangs. This is now a governance crisis on top of a fundamentals story.
