The real test is earnings Tue Nov 17 — the first under interim CEO Colette Kress. Watch $148.80 (Thursday's panic low) as the line; a break below it opens the next air pocket. A reclaim of $180–186 would signal stabilisation. *Analysis only, not financial advice.*
