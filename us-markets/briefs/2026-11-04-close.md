## The day in one line

Election Day brought a tech rebound after Monday's selloff: chips led, most of the universe beat the tape, and Apple was the clear exception — still digesting its margin warning.

## Holdings

### GOOGL — Alphabet: $280.52, +0.55% (prev. close $278.99)
- Range: $277.20–$281.36. A quiet, lagging day: up only half a percent while the Nasdaq rose ~1%.
- No Alphabet-specific catalyst in the day's coverage; the move looks like a partial bounce with the tape rather than a stock-specific re-rating. It remains well off its $408.61 52-week high and close to its $270.70 low — the weakest chart in the universe on a longer view.

### NVDA — Nvidia: $208.33, +3.10% (prev. close $202.06)
- Range: $205.93–$209.10. The day's strongest holding and a clear stock-specific outperformer, ~2 points ahead of the Nasdaq.
- Why: a broad chip rebound after the recent AI-spending rout — Bloomberg's chip gauge rose ~3% — plus positioning ahead of Nvidia's own earnings on November 17. Motley Fool noted the stock sits ~15% below its all-time high despite the bounce, with the November 17 report the next real test of the AI-capex thesis.

### AAPL — Apple: $332.33, −1.77% (prev. close $338.32) — post-earnings, day two
- Range: $326.00–$334.48. The only decliner in the universe, and it underperformed the Nasdaq by nearly 3 points.
- **Post-earnings brief:** Apple reported fiscal Q4 after Monday's close: revenue $114.48 billion (+12%) and EPS $2.10, both beating Wall Street forecasts — a record quarter on the top line. The stock fell anyway, for a second straight session (Monday: −1.83% to $338.32; Tuesday: −1.77% to $332.33), because of guidance: December-quarter gross margin guided to 45–46%, down from 47.9% in September, with management calling memory-price increases unprecedented and worsening.
- Analyst reaction on Tuesday was mixed rather than panicked: Baird raised its target $330 → $340 (Outperform), UBS raised $296 → $340 (Neutral), Guggenheim set $400, Melius set $370, Morgan Stanley trimmed $355 → $350 (Overweight), and Barclays raised $245 → $261 but stayed Underweight. The read-across: the quarter was fine, the margin squeeze is the debate.
- Thesis note: the earnings beat is intact; what changed is the near-term margin trajectory into the holiday quarter. That, not demand, is what the market is repricing.

### MSFT — Microsoft: $520.71, +2.68% (prev. close $507.12)
- Range: $507.83–$521.15. A strong bounce-back from Monday's −2.83% fall, beating the Nasdaq by ~1.7 points.

## Stock-specific vs market-wide

- Market-wide: NVDA, MSFT, META, ASML, TSM and AMZN all rose with a chip/mega-cap rebound on Election Day — that part of the move was the tape, not company news.
- Stock-specific: AAPL's −1.77% was the exception and is directly attributable to its margin guidance; NVDA's +3.10% was the strongest move and reflects both the chip rebound and pre-earnings positioning into November 17.
- GOOGL's +0.55% lagged the tape with no company catalyst — the softest relative performance after Apple.

## Koda's read

The rebound looks like relief, not resolution: chips bounced after a three-day rout, but the two questions that caused the rout — AI-capex payback and, for Apple, memory costs — are unanswered. Election results overnight and Nvidia's November 17 report are the next real catalysts; until then, treat today as a bounce inside a correction, not the end of it.
