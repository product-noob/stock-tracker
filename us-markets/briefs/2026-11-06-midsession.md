## 🚨 Alert 1 — TSM +6.34% ($375.48)

**What happened:** Biggest gainer in the universe, on two drivers at once. First, the broad chip dip-buy (SOX +5%+). Second — and this one is TSM-specific — The Information reported (via Reuters) that TSMC is drawing up plans to produce advanced memory chips, specifically 3D DRAM, **at Nvidia's request**, led by VP Hsu Kuo-chin (advanced packaging). The memory shortage driven by AI demand has pushed prices up and chip designers scrambling for supply; TSMC would be stepping into a market dominated by Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron — whose shares fell on the report. No timeline, no capex figure, no fab plan disclosed; TSMC and Nvidia declined to comment.

**Koda's read:** The bounce part is mechanical — TSM was -10.78% Thursday and oversold. The 3D DRAM story is genuinely interesting: Nvidia asking its foundry to enter memory signals how binding the memory bottleneck has become, and it would widen TSMC's share of the AI hardware wallet. But "considering" is doing a lot of work here — no fabs, no dates, and memory is a cyclical business that would tie TSMC's earnings to a new cycle it has deliberately avoided. The +6.34% prices in the bounce plus an early optionality story, not a confirmed new line. **Lean: hold off adding.** Yesterday's watch zone was $350–360 on stabilization; at $375 the easy entry is gone. Watch for any TSMC/Nvidia confirmation of the 3D DRAM plans — that, not today's tape, is the real signal. *Analysis only, not financial advice.*
