## The tape: Huang is back — the AI rout reverses in a single session

**The news:** Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board member, effective immediately, on a unanimous vote — 11 days after he resigned. The board said it had input from senior levels of the US government and is confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk; President Trump called it "the correct and proper decision." Colette Kress, interim CEO through the Nov 17 earnings call, remains CFO. (Reuters, WSJ, CNN, Investopedia — Nov 23; carried in the Nov 23 mid-session brief.)

**The market:** S&P 500 ~7,255, **+5.0%**; Nasdaq Composite ~24,169, **+6.9%**; Nasdaq-100 ~26,800, **+7.1%**; Dow ~49,700, **+3.3%** — on track for the best day since April 9, 2025, and the Nasdaq-100's biggest one-day % gain since April 2025 (Reuters). The Nasdaq-100 was on track for a >$2 trillion one-day market-value gain (Reuters). S&P Global flash PMIs came in slightly soft (composite 54.6 vs 55.9, a 4-month low; services 54.7 vs 56.1) and did not dent the rally. CrowdStrike's $43.9B all-stock Zscaler deal was the adjacent tape item (ZS +16%, CRWD down). VIX ~30.5, down ~15% on the day from Friday's 35.89.

**Context for the move:** Friday was the 7th straight down day — S&P -3.70% to 6,910.39 (correction, week -8.6%), Nasdaq -5.28% to 22,617.73 (negative for 2026), Nasdaq-100 -5.6% (week -10.4%, worst since 2020). Monday's snapshot **more than erases Friday's plunge**: the S&P (~7,255) and Nasdaq (~24,169) both sit above Thursday's closes (7,175.83 / 23,879.20). The afternoon faded a little from the morning highs — QQQ was +7.1% at 2:25 PM ET vs +6.7% at 2:56 PM — but the turn held.

## Per-name — why it moved, and Koda's read

### NVDA — $164.90, +31.36% (holding)
**Why:** The Huang reinstatement, full stop. The stock had lost a third of its value in two weeks on the leadership vacuum — Friday alone -14.01% to a fresh 52-week low. Today is its biggest one-day gain in 23 years (Yahoo Finance) and it is back near ~$4 trillion in market value, No. 2 behind Apple (~$5.1T, per FinanceFeeds calculations). Analysts moved fast: Bernstein upgraded to Outperform ($200 PT kept), Melius upgraded to Buy ($220); BofA kept Buy at $280, Wedbush Buy at $225.
**Koda's read:** The move is the removal of the leadership discount — the exact overhang flagged in every brief since Nov 9. Fundamentals never changed: the Nov 17 beat-and-raise (Q3 rev $115.84B, Q4 guide ~$130B) still stands, and the stock is still ~32% below its Oct 6 record $243.37. But the probes remain (Reuters Nov 20 — SEC examining accounting issues; California AG subpoena), Dec 1 House testimony is still ahead, and the board's 11-day reversal raises a governance question of its own. **HOLD — do not chase a +31% gap.** A close above $170 confirms the turn; a fade back under $150 says the relief was the whole trade. Next levels: $171.50 (day high), then $180–185.

### TSM — $363.98, +14.20% (watchlist)
**Why:** No TSMC-specific news — pure AI-chain reversal. TSM was the worst-hit name Friday (-9.48%) and is Nvidia's foundry; the Huang return restores confidence in the whole AI buildout chain.
**Koda's read:** Justified as a reversal of Friday's panic, not new TSMC information — Q4 guide ($52.3–54.3B) unchanged, demand "extremely robust." But note the fade: it opened at $385.49 and has given back ~$21 from the high. Still ~25% below its Oct 6 high and below $400. **HOLD on entry — do not chase.** The $380–400 zone is the real test; a hold above $380 into the close is the first sign of a durable turn.
