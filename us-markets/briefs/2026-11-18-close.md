A strong call tonight (credible FY28 growth reaffirmation, clean answers on China/BIS) is what turns this print into a floor; a wobble on guidance or leadership keeps the stock a trading vehicle, not a buy-and-hold, into Wednesday.

## Why it moved

- **Huang broke his silence — and the stock recovered off its lows on it.** In his first statement since resigning, posted on X, Huang said he owns his mistakes, will cooperate with the board's independent investigation, and would be "honored" to return as CEO if the board decides it is in shareholders', employees', the company's and America's best interest. NVDA popped ~5% off its $150.33 intraday low on the statement and closed -0.44% after being down as much as -3.7%. Caveat: the board is not currently considering reinstatement (WSJ/CNBC), and Huang is set for closed-door testimony before three House committees on Dec 1 (Nvidia GC Tim Teter Dec 2). President Trump rejected the 'spy' theory to the White House press.
- **Chips: day two of the Asia-led rout.** TSMC Taipei fell another -2.03% to NT$2,170 and the Taiex stayed weak; the Commerce/BIS interim final rule (licences for remote AI-chip access by China/D:5 firms above 12M TPP, plus shipment licences to Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Brazil) remains the overhang. TSM's ADR broke the $368 support flagged Monday and tagged $353.50. SOX was down ~5% Monday and weak again.
- **Microsoft: the outage story deepened.** Preliminary findings on last week's global Microsoft 365 outage — a bug undetected since July, routine traffic rerouting on Nov 11 cascading into Outlook/Teams/Copilot failures, full service restored Nov 16, final review due Nov 20 — plus the Ignite keynote failing to steady the tape. MSFT broke $500 intraday (low $496.51) for the first time in this slide and closed -2.27%, its second straight day leading the holdings lower. Five-session return now -7.14%. Ex-dividend Thu Nov 19 ($0.98).
- **Macro: steady data, hawkish Fed voices.** Oct retail sales +0.2% headline (as expected), control group +0.6% vs +0.4% forecast — no relief, no damage. St. Louis Fed President Musalem said several rate increases may be needed before spring 2027; a December hike remains ~95% priced (CME FedWatch). Deutsche Bank now expects three more 25bp hikes (Dec, Jan, Mar) to 4.625%.
- **The rest of the tape:** Disney beat (adj EPS $1.77 vs $1.67, revenue $25.95B +16% — flattered by a 53rd week; dividend +33% to $2.00; $3B savings target) but faded after a premarket pop. Home Depot beat comps (+2.2% vs ~1.7% expected) on $43.4B sales but guided cautiously and named CFO Richard McPhail as CEO from Nov 23 — shares fell ~2%. Healthcare was the weakest sector (UnitedHealth, Humana each -4.7% midday); software also weak (Salesforce -5.7%, Workday -5.5%). Anthropic held its $70–78 IPO range in an updated filing; pricing Wed Nov 18, trading Thu Nov 19 (ANT); Bloomberg reported it could price at the top of the range or higher.
