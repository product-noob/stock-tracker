## Why the tape looked the way it did

- **Hot PPI + solid retail sales = no Fed relief:** PPI's +1.3% m/m (biggest miss vs 0.6% exp) plus retail sales doubling estimates (+0.8%) kept Oct 28 hike odds alive (~27%) and Dec near-certain (~89%) — the AI-duration trade stayed toxic (Investopedia; MarketWatch; CNN)
- **Chip rout day 5, but finding buyers late:** SOXX's 8th straight loss, yet TSM recovered from −5.6% intraday to −2.35% and the Nasdaq erased steep losses — final-hour dip-buying met the rotation (WSJ; MarketWatch)
- **Rotation, not capitulation:** money moved into defensives (Nike/consumer, healthcare/pharma at highs, bonds, gold futures +1.79% to $4,181.30) rather than out of equities — Dow +1.08% vs NDX −0.05% (MarketWatch; WSJ)
- **AI-credit anxiety broadened:** CoreWeave −7% stress gauge; Anthropic slowing-growth headlines "latest red flag" (MarketWatch); Barron's: the quarter's mega-cap earnings (AMZN, META, GOOGL) could "make or break stocks' current bull run"
