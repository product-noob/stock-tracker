## Yesterday's earnings — the split

**GOOGL — REPORTED (Wed AMC): a miss, and the market is punishing it.**
Q3 revenue $127.58B (+25% y/y) vs ~$128.2B est; EPS $2.12 vs ~$3.02 cons — the gap includes an $8.66B loss on equity securities (-$0.55 EPS). Search ads $63.3–63.4B missed (~$64.9B est); total ads ~$82.0B vs ~$83.5B est, with management blaming slower search-ad growth on AI Mode monetizing less for now. The bright spot: Google Cloud +103% to $30.7B. The problem: capex $51.4B in the quarter pushed free cash flow to **-$9.07B — a second straight negative quarter** (vs ~breakeven expected). 2026 capex guide unchanged at $195–205B, and CFO Ashkenazi said 2027 spending rises "significantly" plus near-term margin pressure from third-party cloud capacity. First double miss in nearly four years (Reuters). Watch $266.50 below / $325 above.

**MSFT — REPORTED (Wed AMC): a beat, and the contrast trade.**
Q1 FY27 revenue $92.3B (+19%), adj EPS $4.89 vs $4.71 cons; Azure +48% to $33.2B (beat ~$32.3B est). Q2 guide cleared the bar: revenue $96.0–97.1B, Azure +49–50% CC. Capex $45.2B came in below the >$50B expected — but only because third-party capacity deliveries slipped, not because spending was cut; Q2 capex >$50B still guided. Free cash flow $18.6B, ~$10B above expectations. The market's read: AI spend is fine when it converts to cash. Watch $539 / $550.
