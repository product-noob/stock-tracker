# Market-close recap — Wednesday Oct 28, 2026 (4:00 PM ET)

**A hold-and-wait day: the Fed held, the tape barely moved, and the real action starts after the bell.** The FOMC kept rates at **3.75–4.00%** as expected, but the vote was **10–2**, with Beth Hammack and Lisa Cook dissenting in favour of a 25bp hike — Cook's first dissent since joining the Board in 2022. Chair Kevin Warsh offered no forward guidance, and markets still price ~90% odds of a December hike. The reaction was muted: the **S&P 500 rose ~0.2% to ~7,738** and the **Dow ~0.2% to ~52,500**, while the **Nasdaq Composite finished essentially flat** (Barron's live coverage; CNBC via market roundups). The 10-year yield ticked up to ~5.02%. Chips stayed bid on SK Hynix's record quarter (Micron +~4%, AMD +~1.6%), but the mega-caps split: Alphabet rose on an OpenAI chip-buying report while Apple fell despite its new touchscreen MacBook Pro, and Meta kept sliding.

The session's real verdict comes after this recap is written: **GOOGL and MSFT both report after the close** (Alphabet call 4:30 PM ET, Microsoft 5:30 PM ET). Their numbers are not out at bell time, so this is a pre-earnings close recap — the results and reaction get full coverage in tomorrow's pre-market brief.

## Why each name moved

**ASML +1.49% ($1,659.95) — third straight flagged day, still sector beta.** No ASML-specific news; the ADR rode the semiconductor bid sparked by SK Hynix's record results and held well above its ~$1,586 200-day average. The Oct 14 Q3 thesis (2027–28 supply scarcity, FY26 guide raised to EUR 44–45B) is unchanged, and the move is breadth in the chip chain, not new ASML information.

**GOOGL +1.22% ($324.48) — OpenAI chip report lifted it into its own print.** TradingView reported Alphabet gained on news that OpenAI intends to buy Google's AI chips — a notable demand signal for Google's TPUs ahead of the earnings call. The stock held above $320 all session after reclaiming it Tuesday. Earnings after close today: cons adj EPS ~$3.02–3.03, rev ~$127.9–128.2B; watch Cloud growth (Zacks: ~+92% to $29.15B), Search, and capex/FCF against the raised $195–205B 2026 capex forecast.

**AMZN +0.47% ($254.29) — quiet drift into Thursday's earnings.** No company news; it held Tuesday's gain and traded a tight $251.98–255.36 range. Earnings Thu Oct 29 after close: cons EPS ~$2.00–2.01, rev ~$202.1–206.3B; watch AWS growth and margins, plus the ~$220B capex run-rate flagged in previews.

**TSM +0.46% ($438.87) — third straight gain, but the smallest of the three.** No TSMC-specific news; the ADR again outran its home listing and held above the $418 breakout level flagged Monday. The SK Hynix result reinforces the AI-memory demand story that underpins TSMC's Q4 guide ($52.3–54.3B revenue), but today's move is sector drift, not new TSMC information.

**MSFT −0.22% ($530.15) — flat into its own print, as usual.** No fresh company news; a $526.14–534.57 range and a small give-back ahead of earnings. Earnings after close today: cons adj EPS ~$4.71, rev ~$90.6B; watch Azure growth (~45% constant-currency guide) and capex above $50B for the quarter — the call's capacity and margin commentary has mattered more than the headline beat in recent quarters.

**AAPL −0.94% ($343.85) — fell despite the new MacBook Pro.** Apple's "Pro and tell" event (10 AM PT) unveiled the revamped touchscreen MacBook Pro Bloomberg had flagged, but the stock faded from a $347.18 high to close near its $343.71 low — a sell-the-news session, and a give-back of Tuesday's +0.91%. ## Benchmark context

This was a **flat, wait-and-see tape**: S&P +~0.2%, Nasdaq ~flat, Dow +~0.2% — a sharp contrast to Tuesday's chip-led +0.6%/+1.1% advance. Against that tape, ASML and GOOGL beat the benchmarks; AMZN and TSM roughly matched the S&P; MSFT was flat; AAPL, META and NVDA lagged outright. In other words, the chip chain paid for a third day, the ad/AI-platform names did not, and the biggest single-stock moves were de-risking ahead of tonight's earnings, not reactions to news.
