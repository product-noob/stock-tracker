**Session:** Fri Nov 13, 9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET. **Note on timing:** this run fires 1:30 AM IST = 3:00 PM ET, one hour before the 4:00 PM ET close under EST — so this recap was written after the bell using Finnhub's 16:00 EST closing prints, i.e. official closes. % moves vs official Thu Nov 12 4 PM ET closes in the repo. ## Tape
A broad Friday retreat, led by chips — but the week still ended green. The S&P 500 fell 0.75%, the Nasdaq Composite 1.24% and the Nasdaq-100 1.20%, while the Dow slipped just 0.14%. The driver was the same as Thursday's, only wider: Nvidia's leadership crisis kept bleeding (NVDA −2.0%, its worst week in over a year), and this time the succession trade reversed with it — AMD gave back its entire Thursday rally (−8.8%) and Broadcom fell 4.8%. Even good news didn't help: Cisco and Applied Materials both beat and raised guidance after Thursday's close, and both fell Friday (−6.2% and −2.7%). The one relief was macro: October PPI came in a touch softer than expected (+0.3% m/m vs +0.4% expected), which trimmed 50bp December-hike odds — but the 10-year still closed at 5.13%, up 27bp on the week. The rotation split held: META (+2.9%) and AAPL (new intraday high) were the day's winners while the AI complex sold off. For the week, the S&P 500 still gained 2.15%, the Nasdaq 1.92% and the Dow 1.83%.

## Why it moved

### NVDA — $162.85, -2.01% (holding)
Day two of the sell-off, with no fresh company news — the street kept cutting. Barclays lowered its target to $210 from $275 (kept Overweight); Truist cut to Hold from Buy with its target slashed to $174 from $346, citing the leadership gap, workplace culture and reports of Nvidia processors reaching Chinese entities; HSBC downgraded to Hold with a $170 target. The stock opened at $169.91, briefly touched $170, then slid to a $158.39 low before recovering into the close — still its worst week in over a year (−10.3% over five sessions, per Finnhub) and, per Reuters, its longest weekly losing streak since October 2012. It is now −26.4% over the past month, −33% below the Oct 6 high of $243.37, and −12.7% year-to-date; market cap is ~$3.9T, below $4T. Volume ran ~1.6–1.7x average.

**Koda's read:** The panic is over, the bleed isn't — a −2% follow-through day on heavy volume with three more target cuts says the Street is still re-pricing the post-Huang company, not buying the dip. The next hard data point is Tuesday Nov 17 earnings (cons EPS ~$2.52, rev ~$111.2B), the first results under interim CEO Kress. **Lean: HOLD — don't sell into the slide, and don't buy yet either.** Watch $158.39 (Friday's low) below and $170 above; $148.80 (Thursday's low) is the line that matters.

### GOOGL — $295.77, -0.98% (holding)
The rotation bid reversed. After two green days totalling ~+3.9% on Nvidia money, Google gave back about a point, fading from a $304.97 high to close below $300. No fresh company news — it was a tape day, and the TPU story didn't insulate it once the whole AI complex turned down.

**Koda's read:** A give-back, not a break — but $300 failed again as resistance, and the close under it keeps the stock in no-man's-land. **Lean: HOLD — don't chase.** $293.44 (Friday's low) is support; a close back above $300 with volume is the first real test. Still ~−15% over the past month and ~28% below the $408.61 52-week high.

### AAPL — $356.27, +0.40% (holding)
Another new intraday all-time high — $360.83 — then a fade to close near the day's low of $355.75. The shape says the same thing as Thursday: Apple is the safe-haven bid in a nervous tape (Foxconn pointed to resilient premium iPhone demand, per the mid-session brief), but buyers aren't chasing it above $360 yet. No fresh company news; KeyCorp's Underweight reiteration from Thursday still stands as the Street's lone bear flag.

**Koda's read:** Strength with a ceiling — new highs two days running, but both days closed well off the top. **Lean: HOLD.** $355.75 is now the level to hold; a close above $360.83 confirms the breakout. Next report Jan 27, 2027.

### MSFT — $523.88, +0.17% (holding)
Quiet and green, again the steadiest mega-cap. The one wrinkle: Microsoft's global services outage entered a second day, with services only gradually recovering from around 12:30 PM ET (CNBC, via SellTheNews) — the stock dipped to $513.82 intraday and still closed up. The Anthropic-Office scare that cost it −4.5% earlier in the week is not re-accelerating.

**Koda's read:** Resilience is the story — an outage plus a down tape and it still closed higher, holding above $520. **Lean: HOLD.** $513.82 is support; $526.42 (Friday's high) is resistance. Ex-dividend Nov 19 ($0.98).

### META — $641.69, +2.90% (watchlist)
The day's winner in the universe, on no company news at all — the best kind of rotation signal. Meta has now gained ~6% over five sessions, reclaimed $640, and is the only watchlist name clearly repairing its chart. The background is unchanged (the infrastructure-chief departure, the debt-funded buildout debate), but with Nvidia and the chip complex selling off, money is parking in the non-AI mega-cap.

**Koda's read:** A rotation winner, not a re-rating — +2.9% on no news means the tape is doing the work, so don't over-read it. **Lean: WATCHLIST — constructive;** $624.79 is support; $643.91 (Friday's high) is the next test. Still ~−13% over the past month.

### AMZN — $266.64, -0.30% (watchlist)

**Koda's read:** Drifting with the tape, nothing stock-specific. **Lean: WATCHLIST — constructive on AWS, patient on price;** $263.91 is support; $270 is the next test.

### TSM — $398.73, -2.02% (watchlist)
NVDA contagion, day two — and this time it closed below $400, the level it had defended all week. The stock opened at $407.60, sank to $391.50, and recovered only part of it. No company news; the demand story is intact (October revenue NT$586.89B, +59.7% y/y, the biggest month ever; dividend raised to NT$9 from NT$7), but the tape didn't care.

**Koda's read:** The $400 break matters — it's the first close below the line since the sell-off began, and it came on contagion, not fundamentals. **Lean: WATCHLIST — constructive, but the $400 line is now resistance, not support;** $391.50 is the marker below. A reclaim of $400 on Monday repairs the damage.

### ASML — $1,495.16, -0.13% (watchlist)
The best-behaved chip name on a brutal chip day — essentially flat while AMD fell 8.8% and the SOX dropped ~4.5% by midday. It traded as low as $1,462.32 and recovered to close just under $1,500.
