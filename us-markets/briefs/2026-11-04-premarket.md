## AMD after the close — the chip read-through

AMD reported Q3 after Tuesday's close: revenue $13.38B (+45% y/y, a record, vs ~$13.1B consensus), non-GAAP EPS $2.09 (vs ~$1.94–1.95), data-centre revenue $8.55B (+97% y/y, 64% of sales). Q4 guide ~$17B (±3%, vs ~$16.3B expected). The bigger move: CEO Lisa Su said 2027 revenue should grow more than 80%, with server revenue roughly doubling and data-centre AI growing faster still. AMD rose 6–7% pre-market; Arista also beat and raised guidance (+6%). That is a genuine AI-demand signal — but NVDA, TSM and ASML not following it pre-market says the market is still discriminating between AI winners and AI spenders/equipment, not buying the whole chain.
