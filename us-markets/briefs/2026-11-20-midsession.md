## What happened — the AI trade is unwinding, day 7

This is not a single-stock story; it is a global chip/AI liquidation. Overnight Asia led the rout: Taiwan's Taiex −6.2% (worst week since 1990, −17.5% on the week), Kospi −5.2%, Nikkei −5%+, with TSMC Taipei falling below NT$2,000 for the first time since April. Europe followed (ASML −6%+, continental tech basket −4%), Nasdaq-100 futures were −1.88% pre-market, and Treasuries rallied as money fled to safety.

Fresh drivers today (US sources only):

- **China chip curbs tighten (FT, Thu):** TSMC and Samsung have suspended certain chip shipments to customers in China, and the US Commerce Department moved to close the loophole letting Chinese firms access Nvidia's most powerful AI chips remotely. This is the most direct hit on TSM/NVDA/ASML today.
- **Anthropic IPO failed to revive sentiment:** ANT, up 17% on its Thu debut, fell 6%+ in pre-market; Morningstar/Barchart note the IPO may have drained liquidity from the rest of the AI trade instead of validating it.
- **Fed tightening back on the table:** Oct FOMC minutes showed nearly all policymakers expecting further tightening; a Dec hike is ~95% priced, and UBS now expects +25bp in Dec and again in March. Higher rates are the slow poison for the debt-funded AI buildout story.
- **NVDA-specific overhang persists:** CA Attorney General Bonta served an investigative subpoena Thu (chips reaching China / whether investors were misled); the Huang probe/CEO search remains unresolved ahead of the Dec 1 House testimony.
