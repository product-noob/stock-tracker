## NVDA pre-earnings brief — reports TODAY after the close

- **When:** results ~4:20 PM ET, call 5:00 PM ET. First report under interim CEO Colette Kress (also CFO) since Jensen Huang resigned Nov 11/12 after 33 years as CEO.
- **Consensus:** EPS $2.47–2.52, revenue ~$108.7–111.2B (Finnhub: $2.523 / $111.2B; Webull-compiled: $2.47 / ~$108.7B). Company guide was $108B ±2%. Last quarter: revenue $96.2B, EPS $2.22, data-center $89B (92.7% of sales).
- **What to watch:** (1) FY2028 growth guidance — management had pencilled in ~70%, described as supply-constrained; (2) whether Kress can reassure on leadership continuity and the product roadmap; (3) data-center demand and gross margin; (4) any comment on China/Megaspeed and the new BIS rules; (5) buyback. Morgan Stanley's view: the quarter itself is already digested — the call is what moves the stock.
- **The governance overhang:** Treasury Secretary Bessent said Sunday the board should hire an outside CEO ("dramatic shakeup") and answer export-compliance questions within 10 days (~Nov 25); Megaspeed's Malaysian subsidiary allegedly shipped ≥$7.5B of Nvidia chips to China over two years. The board announced TODAY a new National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee (chaired by Suzanne Nora Johnson). The board is reportedly not considering bringing Huang back; Arm CEO Rene Haas has reportedly been approached. Congress wants Huang for a closed-door interview Dec 1 and Senate testimony Dec 3.
- **Positioning:** options imply ~7–8.6% move (~$145–166 range). PT cuts into the print: UBS $300→$240, Mizuho $250→$220, TD Cowen $275→$200, CLSA $255. Stock is ~36% below its Oct 6 high of $243.37 and ~1 month -27%.
- **Koda's read:** HOLD — do not sell into the print and do not buy the dip ahead of it. The print is a binary event; the call matters more than the quarter.
