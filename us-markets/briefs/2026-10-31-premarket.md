## 📊 Pre-earnings brief: AAPL — Monday Nov 2, after close (call 5:00 PM ET)

The week's main event — and the one mega-cap without an AI-capex problem reports into a tape that has just punished every name that does have one.

- **Consensus:** EPS ~$1.99 (Zacks $2.00) on revenue ~$113.3B (Zacks $112.95B, MarketBeat $113.39B) — about +7.6% EPS and +10.6% revenue vs a year ago ($1.85 / $102.47B). Apple has beaten EPS consensus in each of its last 8 reports.
- **Apple's own guide (from the Jul 30 call):** revenue growth +9% to +11% (~$111.7–113.7B) and gross margin 47–48%, including ~1 point of tariff refunds — so ex-refunds the underlying margin guide is ~46–47%, vs 48.1% in the June quarter.
- **What to watch:** (1) The December-quarter guide — the first outlook from new CEO John Ternus (succeeded Tim Cook on Sep 1), for a holiday quarter that carried $85.3B of iPhone a year ago, launched without a standard iPhone. (2) Memory costs — DRAM/NAND prices rose in March, June and again in September as AI data centers compete for capacity; inventory doubled to $11.09B in nine months, and Apple says that carry-in benefit fades after September. (3) iPhone vs Services — iPhone Duo demand was called "extraordinary" with shipping into Jan 2027; Services momentum has to offset any iPhone softness. (4) The margin read: consensus sits near the top of Apple's revenue guide, so a midpoint print could still read as a miss.
- **Levels:** Friday's range was $334.35–$349.19; the stock held well above its ~$332 50-day average and closed ~$344 — the most resilient mega-cap into earnings.
