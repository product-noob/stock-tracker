## Why it moved

### NVDA — $176.74, -5.30% (holding)
The board probe into CEO Jensen Huang (FT report Nov 7; House committee chairs want a closed-door briefing Nov 18, Nvidia to answer questions by Wed Nov 11) is the whole story: NVDA is the only major chip name down while AMD +11%, Broadcom +7.7%, Micron +5.9% and SOXX +5.7%. Volume ~2.4x average confirms real selling, not drift. Goldman reiterated Buy ($300 PT) today. Motley Fool notes the forward P/E is now ~12x on analyst expectations. Nothing in the reports touches demand, Blackwell shipments, or the Nov 17 earnings print.

**Koda's read:** A governance/leadership risk discount, not a business event — the -5.3% looks overdone on fundamentals alone, but the overhang is real until the board review and the Nov 18 briefing clarify things, and headlines can keep coming. **Lean: HOLD — do not add into the headline storm.** Watch $172–175 as the test, $164 below; $181–185 is now resistance. Earnings Nov 17 is the next real catalyst.

### ASML — $1,510.27, +6.00% (watchlist)
Second leg of Friday's +5.02% bounce on the chip rally; Amsterdam listing closed +4.7% at €1,343.80. One fresh item: ASML published a buyback transaction report today (GlobeNewswire) — shares repurchased on each trading day Nov 2–6. The 2027–28 supply-shortage thesis (CEO, Oct 14 call) is unchanged.

**Koda's read:** A bounce, not a re-rating — ASML is still well below its 50-day average and the move carries no new fundamental information beyond the buyback update. **Lean: WATCH — do not chase.** The ~$1,450 stabilisation test is now cleared; a pullback toward $1,450–1,480 is the better entry zone.

### META — $641.46, +5.13% (watchlist)
JPMorgan added Meta to its Analyst Focus List, replacing Alphabet, keeping Overweight and a $940 PT. Analyst Doug Anmuth (via CNBC): post-Q3, Meta's ad strength is "more evident than ever" — Q3 ad revenue +26% to $63.1B, ~$13B of new ad dollars, roughly twice what Google Search added; JPMorgan expects Meta's Family of Apps ad revenue to pass Google Search from Q4. **Koda's read:** A real analyst catalyst on a real Q3 beat — direction justified, though +5% in a day on a list change is rich. The debt-funded buildout overhang hasn't gone away, but the ad engine is now visibly out-earning Google's. **Lean: WATCHLIST — no chase at $641;** $600 is support; a close above $650 would confirm momentum.
