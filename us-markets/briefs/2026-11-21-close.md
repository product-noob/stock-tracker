## The day's story: the Anthropic debut didn't save the trade — it buried it

**Anthropic (ANT) fell ~12–13% to ~$85 on its first full day of trading** (Fool: -13.34% midday; Fast Company: -12.4% to $85.16; Barron's: sank 11%), barely above the $83 IPO price after opening at $111.50 on Thursday and closing at $97.17. The largest IPO on record ($81.3B) was the week's big test of whether the AI trade could recover; instead it became the accelerant. The Nasdaq, at all-time highs six weeks ago, has now given back the whole year.

**Why the selling keeps deepening — four drivers:**
1. **The governance discount on Nvidia is now the market's problem, not just Nvidia's.** NVDA is down ~30% since the Jensen Huang resignation reports surfaced on Nov 12 (Fool's math: -26% as of ~1 PM ET at -6.6% on the day; the afternoon slide takes it to ~-30%). The quarter was extraordinary — Q3 FY27 revenue $115.8B, +103% y/y; data-centre $107.6B, +110%; Q4 guide ~$130B — and the stock has fallen every session since reporting. The overhang stack from the week: CA AG subpoena (Thu), Moody's cutting the credit outlook to Stable from Positive, Bloomberg reporting the board has approached Arm CEO Rene Haas as a possible successor, and the WSJ's "$500B of AI-financing pledges, not a dollar deployed".
2. **Wedbush's Dan Ives called it a "perfect storm"** (Fool): the Democrats' midterm win raising fears of tougher AI regulation and data-centre restrictions; Huang's departure; forced selling and fund losses that have shrunk the market's ability to buy the dip; and the Anthropic IPO itself pulling money out of existing tech names into the new listing. His counterpoint, worth keeping: demand numbers from Nvidia, Google Cloud and Anthropic's own prospectus ($110B+ committed to Amazon, $121.3B to Google in long-term cloud deals) still show the buildout happening.
3. **The circular-financing question went mainstream.** The ECB's November Financial Stability Review flagged growing interconnections and circular financing arrangements across AI companies (TradingView) — the same worry behind the WSJ's "not a dollar deployed" reporting.
4. **Rates are the brake, and the Fed won't lift its foot.** December hike ~95% priced; Jefferson and Goolsbee's AI-inflation warnings from Thursday still frame the tape. The 2Y yield falling 12.6bp today despite the hike pricing says the bond market is buying safety, not pricing easier money.

## Koda's read

This stopped being a valuation debate two days ago. When a company reports revenue up 103% and guides to $130B next quarter and falls 12% the same week, the market is pricing leadership and trust, not demand — and the Anthropic IPO, meant to be the proof point, is now trading at its IPO price. Everything in the universe is a HOLD into this tape: AAPL and GOOGL are the only names showing relative strength, NVDA's capitulation volume (2.4x average) is the one number that could mark a washout — but volume alone doesn't bottom a stock with an unresolved CEO question. The S&P sitting ~10% off its high with the Nasdaq negative for the year says this is a market event now, not a stock event. Don't chase the bounce until NVDA prints a day that isn't a new low.
