## The day's story: Anthropic IPO opens, then the tape fades

Every one of the 8 names opened higher and faded; GOOGL and NVDA gave back ~3.5–4.8pts of opening gains, ASML and TSM faded to their day lows. The pattern reads as Anthropic enthusiasm meeting the same tape that has sold every AI rally for two weeks — hawkish FOMC minutes (Dec hike odds ~95% for 25bp, 50bp still discussed) and 10Y yields ~5.07–5.12%.
