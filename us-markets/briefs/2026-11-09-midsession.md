House committee chairs want a closed-door briefing Nov 18; Nvidia is to answer questions by Wed Nov 11 (CNBC). The stock is the only big chip name down while AMD +11%, Broadcom +7.7%, Micron +5.9% and SOXX +5.7% — this is a company-specific move, not sector. Goldman reiterated Buy ($300 PT) today.
