## ⚠️ NVDA +4.03% — dip-buying bounce, probe unresolved

**What happened:** After a two-day rout (−9.5%, ~$430B of market value lost Mon–Tue) on the Jensen Huang probe, buyers stepped in. The stock gapped to ~$177.70 premarket and held ~$175.76 midday (day high $176.27). The bounce rides the CoreWeave-led chip rally, not any resolution: the board's Wachtell Lipton independent review (led by former acting US Attorney Joon H. Kim) is ongoing, the board says it has no reason so far to believe secrets or national security were compromised, and Sen. Kevin Cramer told Politico late Tuesday that Huang should resign, calling the extortion risk "tremendous." Today is Huang's deadline to tell Congress whether he will answer questions; House briefing is Nov 18, earnings Nov 17. Morgan Stanley reiterated Overweight, $300 PT (MarketBeat, today); Baird cut to Neutral, PT $500→$200, citing limited 2027 data-centre visibility and uncertainty around Huang's future.

**Koda's read:** The +4% is a relief bounce off an oversold tape (RSI ~24), not a verdict — the stock is still ~28% below its Oct 6 high of $243.37 and the governance overhang is live until Nov 17–18. The move looks *understandable*, not yet justified as a turn. **Hold — don't chase the bounce.** $164.27 (52-wk low) is the line below; a reclaim of ~$180–186 would start to signal stabilisation. Earnings Nov 17 is the real test.

## ⚠️ TSM +4.70% — board news + record October sales

**What happened:** Taipei shares closed +4.81% at NT$2,395 and the ADR followed. On Tuesday TSMC's board approved (a) up to $500M each in Anthropic (at IPO price) and OpenAI (preferred stock), (b) $40.9B of new capex for advanced capacity/packaging/R&D, and (c) a record Q3 cash dividend of NT$9/share (up from NT$7). October sales hit NT$586.89B, +59.7% y/y and +14.7% m/m — a new monthly record. Foreign investors net-bought TSMC for the first time after a 23-session selling streak (Focus Taiwan/CNA; TipRanks; Stocktwits).

**Koda's read:** The move looks *justified* — this is real, company-specific good news on top of a tape rebound, and TSMC is the highest-quality name in the universe. But +4.7% in a day, on top of +2% Tuesday, is a lot to chase. **Entry-timing view: don't chase; a pullback toward ~$400 is the cleaner entry.** If already watching, a starter position on weakness is reasonable; the story (AI demand, pricing power) is intact.

## ⚠️ MSFT −2.54% — the one name moving against the tape

**What happened:** MSFT opened ~$534 and slid to a day low of $518.45, testing its 100-hour moving average ($518.11) and the $513.50–517.60 swing area (InvestingLive). The driver is the Anthropic software scare: Anthropic unveiled Claude Sheets (an Excel-like spreadsheet) plus whiteboard/office products on Tuesday, and MSFT fell 2.2% then and another ~2.5% today — while the Nasdaq is up ~2%. Microsoft also warned a proposed US ban on Chinese optical transceivers could raise AI buildout costs. **Koda's read:** The divergence is real, but the scare looks *overdone* on fundamentals: Q1 FY27 FCF was $18.6B and Azure grew +48% — Microsoft is the hyperscaler that can fund its AI buildout from cash flow. The pattern rhymes with February's similar AI-software scare (fell 2.2%, then slid ~14% before recovering), so near-term downside isn't exhausted. **Hold — don't sell into the scare.** A break below $513.50 opens more downside; stabilisation above ~$520–530 would be the first sign the selling is done. This is a buy-the-weakness candidate, not a sell — but wait for the tape to confirm.
