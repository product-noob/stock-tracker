## Pre-earnings brief: AAPL — Monday, November 2, after the close (call 5:00 PM ET)

AAPL is the only universe name reporting within the next 2 trading days, and it is the week's main event.

- **Consensus:** EPS ~$1.99 (Zacks $2.00, MarketBeat $1.99), revenue ~$113.3–113.4B — up ~7.6% and ~10.7% y/y respectively, vs $1.85 / $102.47B a year ago.
- **Apple's own guide (from the July call):** revenue growth 9–11% (~$111.7–113.7B), gross margin 47–48% (including ~1 point of tariff refunds), operating expenses $19.1–19.4B.
- **What to watch:** (1) The December-quarter guide — the first from new CEO John Ternus (in role since Sept 1) and the first holiday outlook, for a quarter that did $85.3B of iPhone a year ago. (2) Gross margin ex-tariff-refunds: the guide implies ~46–47% clean, vs 48.1% in June — memory-cost pressure is the swing factor. (3) iPhone: mid-teens growth expected (~$49B a year ago), including early iPhone 18 Pro sales; iPhone Duo (launched Oct 23) falls in the *next* quarter. (4) Services growth and whether the tariff-refund benefit is fading. Note the bar: Apple has beaten EPS consensus in each of its last 8 reports, yet fell 7.35% the session after July's report — a beat alone may not be enough.
