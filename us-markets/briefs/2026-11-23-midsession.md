## The tape: Huang is back — the AI rout reverses in one morning

**The news:** Nvidia's board announced before the open that Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and CEO and as a board member, effective immediately — a unanimous vote, 11 days after he resigned. The board said it received input from senior levels of the US government and is confident the reinstatement poses no national security risk; President Trump called it "the correct and proper decision." This reverses the board's own position on the Nov 17 earnings call, when chair Harvey Jones said the board "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." Colette Kress, interim CEO through the earnings call, remains CFO. (Reuters, WSJ, CNN, Investopedia, Barron's — Nov 23.)

**The market:** Nasdaq +6.8%, S&P 500 +5.1%, Dow +3.3% (~1,600 points) — on track for the best day since April 9, 2025. Nasdaq-100 +6.6%, on track for a >$2 trillion one-day market-value gain (Reuters) — its biggest one-day % gain since April 2025. The Nasdaq's +7.3% opening jump was its biggest opening gain on record back to 1991 (Dow Jones Market Data via MarketWatch). The bounce was already forming pre-market (futures S&P +2.4%, Nasdaq-100 +3.0% — dip-buying after Friday's rout); the Huang announcement turbocharged it. S&P Global flash PMIs came in slightly soft (composite 54.6 vs 55.9, a 4-month low; services 54.7 vs 56.1; manufacturing 54.5 in line) and did not dent the rally. Bitcoin ~$62,600 after dipping below $58,000 Friday. CrowdStrike's $43.9B all-stock Zscaler deal is the adjacent tape item (ZS +16%, CRWD down).

**Context for the move:** Friday was the 7th straight down day — S&P -3.70% to 6,910.39 (correction, week -8.6%), Nasdaq -5.28% to 22,617.73 (negative for 2026), Nasdaq-100 -5.6% (week -10.4%, worst since 2020). Today's rebound does not yet reclaim Friday's loss, let alone the week's: the S&P is still ~4% below Thursday's close.

## Flagged names — what happened, and Koda's read

### NVDA — $164.46, +31.0% (holding) ⚠️
**What happened:** The board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, president and board member effective immediately, unanimous vote, after input from senior US government levels and confidence there is no national security risk. Trump welcomed it. The stock had lost a third of its value in two weeks on the leadership vacuum — Friday alone it fell 14.01% to $125.53, a fresh 52-week low ($124.50 intraday).
**Koda's read:** The move is justified as the removal of the leadership discount — the exact overhang flagged in every brief since Nov 9. Fundamentals never changed: Nov 17 beat-and-raise (Q3 rev $115.84B, Q4 guide ~$130B) still stands, and the stock is still ~32% below its Oct 6 record $243.37. But: Barron's notes the probes remain (Reuters Nov 20 — SEC examining accounting issues; California AG subpoena), the Dec 1 House testimony is still ahead, and the board's 11-day reversal (resign → "does not intend to reinstate" → reinstated) raises a governance question of its own. **HOLD — do not chase a +31% gap.** The leadership discount is gone; the next levels are $171.50 (day high), then $180–185. A close above $170 would confirm the turn; a fade back under $150 would say the relief was the whole trade.
