## ⚠️ ALERT 1 — NVDA: the beat-and-raise that sold off

**What happened:** Nvidia reported a genuinely huge Q3 after the close Tue — revenue $115.84B (+103% YoY) vs ~$109B consensus, adj EPS $2.68 vs ~$2.47, Data Center $107.6B, Q4 guide $127.4–132.6B (mid ~$130B) vs ~$125.3B consensus, FY28 revenue growth raised to ~75%, plus a $150B buyback. Shares opened +2.4% at $159.19, touched $159.70, then reversed hard — now $151.04, -2.84% on the day and ~$230B of market value erased from the post-earnings peak (Market Flux). The stock is ~36% below its Oct 6 high and sitting ~$2 above the Nov 12 52-week low of $148.80.

**Why it's falling anyway:** the numbers aren't the problem — governance is. This was the first print since Jensen Huang resigned (Nov 12); interim CEO Colette Kress ran the call, the board is running a CEO search, and Huang is due for a closed-door House briefing today. The WSJ reports the SEC is probing Nvidia, and new Commerce/BIS rules limit remote access to Nvidia hardware in China. Morningstar's Brian Colello: "We're at a point of peak uncertainty because investors still don't know what Nvidia's long-term leadership will look like." Wall Street is split — some analysts upgraded to Strong Buy (one PT $278.60), others downgraded on the China rules + loss of Huang.

**Koda's read:** The quarter was a beat-and-raise on every line that matters, and at ~19.5x trailing earnings the stock is the cheapest it's been in years — but the market is pricing a leadership discount, not a demand problem, and that discount doesn't lift until the CEO question gets an answer. The move looks overdone on fundamentals, justified on uncertainty. **Lean: HOLD.** Don't sell into a governance-driven washout after a beat-and-raise, and don't buy the dip ahead of clarity. The line is $148.80 (Nov 12 low) — a hold there keeps the bottoming case alive; a break below opens the next leg down. Next catalysts: House briefing today, CEO-search news, Anthropic's debut tomorrow.

The capex debate (~$220B this year, more in 2027) is eased, not settled.
