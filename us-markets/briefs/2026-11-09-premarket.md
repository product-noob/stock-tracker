## The Nvidia story — the day's dominant driver

- On Sunday Nov 8, Nvidia's board opened an **independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang**, overseen by a committee of independent directors with outside counsel. It follows NYT/FT reporting that US intelligence believes Alice Huang — a former senior executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data-centre firm and Nvidia customer — was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security to gather intelligence on Huang and influence Nvidia's advanced-chip sales to China.
- Huang admitted in a Sunday memo to employees that he was unfaithful to his wife and apologised to family, staff and shareholders. He remains CEO and says he will cooperate fully.
- The board says it has "no reason to believe" national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, but the inquiry "will examine all available information." No charges, no public finding of wrongdoing. Commerce referred the Megaspeed matter to the Justice Department.
- House committee chairs have asked Huang for a closed-door briefing on **Nov 18**; Nvidia is to answer by **Wed Nov 11** (CNBC). Bernstein maintained Outperform, $400 PT, today.
- Market reaction is NVDA-specific, not sector-wide: AMD +4.67% on an Oppenheimer upgrade, ASML +3.7%, TSM +1.7%, SOXX +0.6%. Dan Ives (Wedbush) called the probe a governance overhang, not a fundamentals event — for now.
- **Levels:** pre-market low ~$175.89; $175–180 is the immediate reference area, $181–185 the floor from last week, $195–200 resistance above. Earnings Tue Nov 17 — the probe will dominate the call.
